\# Product Performance Analysis



\## Business Question



Which products generate the most revenue and profit?



\## Key Findings



\- \*\*The Original Mr. Fuzzy\*\* was the clear volume and revenue leader, with \*\*24,226 units sold\*\* and approximately \*\*$1.15 million in net revenue\*\*.

\- The Original Mr. Fuzzy generated approximately \*\*$677,055 in gross profit\*\*, with a profit margin of \*\*58.91%\*\*.

\- \*\*The Forever Love Bear\*\* generated approximately \*\*$339,963 in net revenue\*\* and \*\*$209,611 in gross profit\*\*, with a \*\*61.66% profit margin\*\*.

\- \*\*The Birthday Sugar Panda\*\* generated approximately \*\*$215,417 in net revenue\*\* and \*\*$143,185 in gross profit\*\*, achieving a \*\*66.47% profit margin\*\*.

\- \*\*The Hudson River Mini Bear\*\* generated approximately \*\*$148,570 in net revenue\*\* and \*\*$100,950 in gross profit\*\*, but had the \*\*highest profit margin at 67.95%\*\*.

\- The analysis shows that the product with the highest sales volume is not necessarily the product with the highest profitability.



\## Business Interpretation



The Original Mr. Fuzzy is the core product in terms of sales volume, revenue, and total profit. Its high sales volume makes it an important product for overall business performance.



However, the Hudson River Mini Bear has the highest profit margin despite having substantially lower sales volume. The Birthday Sugar Panda also demonstrates strong profitability with a 66.47% margin.



This suggests that product performance should be evaluated using multiple KPIs rather than sales volume or revenue alone.



\## Key Takeaway



> \*\*The Original Mr. Fuzzy is the main revenue and volume driver, while the Hudson River Mini Bear has the highest profit margin. The business therefore has an opportunity to balance high-volume products with higher-margin products.\*\*



\## Further Analysis



Further analysis could investigate product performance over time, refund rates by product, and whether certain marketing channels or customer segments perform better for specific products.
