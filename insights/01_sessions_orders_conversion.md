\# Sessions, Orders \& Conversion Rate



\## Business Question



What is the trend in website sessions and order volume, and how has the session-to-order conversion rate changed?



\## Key Findings



\- Monthly website sessions increased substantially from March 2012 to March 2015.

\- Monthly orders increased from 60 in March 2012 to 1,254 in March 2015.

\- Session-to-order conversion rate increased from 3.19% in March 2012 to 8.31% in March 2015.

\- The highest monthly conversion rate in the analyzed period was 8.70% in February 2015.

\- The increase in conversion rate indicates that the business became more effective at converting website sessions into orders over time.



\## Business Interpretation



The data shows strong growth in both website traffic and order volume. More importantly, orders grew faster than sessions, while conversion rate improved considerably.



Further analysis of marketing channels, campaigns, device types, and website behavior is needed to identify the factors contributing to the improvement in conversion.
