\# Revenue Analysis



\## Business Questions



1\. How has revenue per order evolved?

2\. How has revenue per session evolved?

3\. What impact did refunds have on revenue?



\## Key Findings



\- Net revenue increased from approximately \*\*$2,949 in March 2012 to $76,258 in March 2015\*\*.

\- The highest monthly net revenue was approximately \*\*$139,484 in December 2014\*\*.

\- Net revenue per order increased from \*\*$49.16 in March 2012 to $60.81 in March 2015\*\*, representing an increase of approximately 24%.

\- Net revenue per order reached its highest level of \*\*$63.12 in May 2014\*\*.

\- Net revenue per session increased from \*\*$1.57 in March 2012 to $5.06 in March 2015\*\*, more than tripling over the analysis period.

\- The highest net revenue per session was \*\*$5.28 in February 2015\*\*.

\- Refunds had a noticeable impact on revenue in some months. For example, August 2014 had approximately \*\*$7,818 in refunds\*\*, while September 2014 had approximately \*\*$8,564\*\* in refunds.



\## Business Interpretation



The business experienced significant improvement in revenue performance over time. Growth was not driven only by an increase in website traffic and order volume.



Customers also generated more revenue per order, while the amount of net revenue generated per website session increased substantially.



This suggests an overall improvement in the effectiveness of the e-commerce business in converting website traffic into revenue.



Refunds should also be monitored because they can significantly reduce realized revenue in certain periods.



\## Key Takeaway



> \*\*The business became more effective at generating revenue from its website traffic over time. Net revenue per order increased by approximately 24%, while net revenue per session more than tripled between March 2012 and March 2015.\*\*



\## Further Analysis



Further investigation should examine whether changes in marketing channels, product mix, device type, and website behavior contributed to the improvement in revenue efficiency.
